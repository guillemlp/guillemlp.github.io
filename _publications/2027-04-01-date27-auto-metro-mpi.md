Accepted at DATE 2027. This paper automates Metro-MPI partitioning inside Verilator, the open-source Verilog-to-C++ compiler, so large RTL designs can be split into parallel MPI simulation processes without manual partitioning. It is the outcome of a Google Summer of Code 2025 project with the FOSSi Foundation that I mentored.
